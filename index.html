Can't connect?

Practice

Try a stage against a dummy of every other champion. Press Esc to come back.

Champion

Everyone brawls the same way: punch, grab, headbutt, lift, climb. Your champion adds two abilities on top.

Controls

Click a key to change it, then press the new key or mouse button. Esc cancels, Backspace clears.

Tap a hand to punch with it, hold it to grab. Grab someone with both hands and hold Lift to raise them overhead, then let go of the hands to throw. Hanging off a ledge, hold Jump to push yourself up, then push toward the ledge to climb over.

Gamepad: LT/LB left hand, RT/RB right hand, A jump, B lie down, X and Y abilities, left stick click lift, right stick click headbutt.
